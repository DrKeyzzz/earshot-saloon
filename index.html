How to play

  1. The dealer plays their card, then a mystery note. Four cards flip over. Pick the card that shows the mystery note.
  2. Right answers win chips, and a streak multiplies them. Wrong answers cost a chip.
  3. Every town has 6 hands, a visit from Crooked Clara (is she playing fair?), then a High Noon showdown with an Interval Outlaw. Win it to lasso the outlaw and stamp their Wanted poster.
  4. Press your luck: after each town, cash out to keep your chips as your score, or ride on for bigger stakes. Go bust and you keep only half your best.
  5. Stuck on an interval? Visit Hank's Songbook to hear each one with a song you know.
